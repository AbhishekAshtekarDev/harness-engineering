# HT-142: Weekly streak

As a user with a weekly target (for example "gym 3 times a week"), I want to
see how many weeks in a row I met my target, so that I stay motivated even on
habits I do not do every day.

Add `weekly_streak(habit, today)` to `habit_tracker/streaks.py`, following
`docs/product-spec.md`, with unit tests.
